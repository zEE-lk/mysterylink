EVERY TATTOO STARTS WITH A STORY.

Welcome to Mystery Ink Tattoo Studio — where art meets skin. Enosh Fernando runs Mystery Ink the way he posts: quality over quantity. Every piece is drawn for you and treated like an editorial shoot — flowing script, delicate fine line and abstract wave work with clean, deliberate composition. Clients call the studio calm, precise and welcoming, and every one of its 53 Google reviews is 5 stars. Sterile, single-use equipment, every time.

At MysteryLink, every tattoo is treated as an individual piece of artwork. From minimal designs to detailed custom pieces, our goal is to turn your idea into something you can carry with you.
